Our Craft

Baked with Patience & Passion

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery began with a simple mission: bring traditional, slow-fermented bread back to our community table.

We source our grains directly from regional organic farmers and stone-grind them fresh every week. No shortcuts, no artificial additives—just flour, water, salt, and time.
